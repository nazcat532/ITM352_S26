Hello! Welcome to Personalized Gym Routine.

Step 1: Create your profile 
Follow the prompt to enter your detail (Goal, Body Type, Target Areas)

Step 2: View your plan
When the data is entered, the program will create a custom workout list. 
You will be able to view the exercise, sets, and reps on the dashboard.

Step 3: Log your progress
After completing a workout, select "Log Process"
Input the weight used and reps completed.
Submitting on "Save Log" will automatically save data to progress.cvs for future sessions.

Step 4: View chart
Select "View Progress" to generate a visual chart of your fitness journey.
The chart represents the reps and weight progress over time.
